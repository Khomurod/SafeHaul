Privacy Policy

Last updated November 2026 · Rev. E

1. Introduction & Scope

SafeHaul is driver hiring and DOT compliance software for US trucking carriers. Using it means handling sensitive personal information about drivers and applicants, so this policy sets out plainly what we collect, what we do with it, and — just as importantly — what we do not do with it.

SafeHaul is operated by Safehaul Technologies LLC. This Privacy Policy explains how SafeHaul ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, protects, and discloses information. It covers data you provide to us directly, data we collect automatically, data a carrier uploads about its drivers, and data that reaches us from Google or Facebook when you or your company connect them, across our applications and related services.

2. Information We Collect

Account Data (Company & Admin)

When carrier owners, administrators or recruiters set up and use an account, we record the foundational data needed to manage that account:

  • First and last names
  • Work email addresses
  • Company names and physical addresses

Sign in with Google

If you sign in with Google, Google sends SafeHaul your name, email address, Google account identifier and the address of your profile photo. We use them only to sign you in to an account your company created.

A Connected Gmail or Google Workspace Account

If your company connects a Gmail or Google Workspace account so that SafeHaul's emails come from its own address, we store that email address and the permission Google issues to send email from it (a refresh token), encrypted. SafeHaul does not read, store or index the mailbox.

A Connected Facebook Page

If your company connects a Facebook Page to receive leads from its Lead Ads forms, we store the Page's ID and name and the access token Facebook issues for it. For each lead, we import what the person typed into your company's form — such as their name, phone number and email address — and which form and ad they used.

Driver & Applicant Data

Driver hiring involves sensitive records, and SafeHaul stores what a carrier and its applicants put into it. That includes:

  • Commercial Driver's Licence (CDL) details, including a photo of the licence where a driver supplies one
  • Medical Examiner's Certificates and other qualification documents a carrier uploads
  • The application a driver submits, and the hiring stage a recruiter puts them in
  • An application a driver has started but not yet submitted, which the carrier's administrators can see and correct; the driver is shown what they changed before signing. It never holds the driver's Social Security Number or signature
  • Signed documents, and the record of who signed them and when
  • Previous-employment verification requests and the responses employers return

SafeHaul does not order, purchase or retrieve motor vehicle records (MVRs), PSP reports, criminal-history reports or FMCSA Clearinghouse results, and it does not connect to the providers that supply them. If a carrier obtains such a record elsewhere and uploads it to a driver's file, SafeHaul stores that file like any other document — it does not fetch or interpret it.

Automatically Collected Data

When you use the platform we collect certain technical and usage information to keep the service secure and working. This includes:

  • Device identifiers and IP addresses
  • Browser type, session information and timestamps
  • Security cookies and authentication tokens

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the data above to run the hiring and record-keeping work a carrier signed up for:

  • Hiring pipeline: Moving applicants through hiring stages, sorting applications and handling candidate communications.
  • Driver qualification records: Storing the qualification documents a carrier uploads against the right driver, so the file is complete when the carrier needs it. SafeHaul stores and organises these documents and, once a day, tells the carrier's team in the app when a hired driver's recorded valid-until date is near or has passed; it does not remind drivers or email or text anyone about it, and it does not guarantee a carrier's DOT compliance.
  • Previous-employment verification: Sending verification requests to a driver's prior employers and recording the responses they return, with a record of what was asked and answered.
  • Sign-in: Using the details Google sends when you sign in with Google only to sign you in.
  • Email from your company's own address: Using a connected Gmail or Google Workspace account only to send the emails your company's team sends through SafeHaul, and the reminders SafeHaul schedules for them, such as application links, signing requests and previous-employment verification requests.
  • Facebook leads: Adding the leads from your company's Lead Ads forms to your company's lead list, for its recruiters to contact. The Page's access token is used only to receive those leads.
  • Platform operation and improvement: Security monitoring and aggregate, de-identified analysis to keep the service reliable.

4. Data Sharing & Third-Party Disclosure

SafeHaul holds hiring and compliance records on a carrier's behalf, so we keep tight controls on when data leaves the account:

Previous-Employment Verification (PEV)

A core part of SafeHaul is digitising previous-employment verification. In that workflow, specific information about a driver applicant is shared explicitly between the prospective employer and the driver's authorised former employers. An electronic signature acts as the driver's authorisation for that exchange.

Apart from features you use directly (such as PEV) or a valid legal request such as a subpoena or warrant, SafeHaul does not sell, rent or broker driver data to third parties.

5. Google User Data

SafeHaul receives data from Google only when you sign in with Google or your company connects a Gmail or Google Workspace account, and uses it only as described in sections 2 and 3: to sign you in, and to send your company's emails from its own address. SafeHaul does not read, store or index the mailbox, and no one at SafeHaul reads your email.

SafeHaul's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Data from Google APIs is not sold, not used for advertising, and not used to develop, improve or train AI or machine-learning models. It is not shared with third parties, except where the law requires it.

6. Facebook Data and How to Delete It

If your company connects a Facebook Page, SafeHaul holds the Page's ID, name and access token, and the leads imported from your company's Lead Ads forms, as described in section 2. When your company disconnects the Page from SafeHaul, or asks us to, SafeHaul deletes its access token and stops importing its leads.

To have this data deleted (for any other data, see section 8):

  • Email us: write to info@safehaul.io, from your SafeHaul account's address if you have one, naming the Facebook Page or the person whose data you want deleted. We confirm the request and delete the data within 30 days, except records a carrier must keep by law.
  • Remove SafeHaul in Facebook: in Facebook, open Settings → Business integrations and remove SafeHaul. That ends SafeHaul's access to your Page at once.

7. Data Retention & Security

We limit who can reach driver data and keep a record of access. Security controls include:

  • Encryption: Data is encrypted in transit with industry-standard TLS, and sensitive files such as CDL images and signatures are stored with access restricted to the company they belong to.
  • Access controls: Document and verification files are never public URLs. Every link is issued by our server for a single request and checked against company membership before the file is released.
  • Audit records: Actions taken on a driver record are logged, and completed signed documents are sealed so later alteration is detectable.

We retain data for as long as a carrier maintains an active account, or as required under the DOT driver-qualification recordkeeping schedules that apply to the carrier. We accommodate deletion requests where we can, subject to those retention obligations, following a verified administrative request.

We keep a Gmail or Facebook Page connection's token only while the connection is in place. When your company disconnects Gmail or a Facebook Page, or asks us to, we delete the stored token, and a Gmail token is also revoked with Google.

8. Your Rights

Subject to verifying your identity and to the laws that apply to you (such as GDPR or CCPA), you or the individuals whose data a carrier holds may:

  • Request access to the personal data held about you in the application.
  • Ask us to correct or amend data that is factually inaccurate.
  • Request deletion of a profile ("Right to Be Forgotten"), subject to the federal DOT retention periods that may bind the carrier employing you.

Questions about this policy or about the data we hold can be sent to us directly, and we will route and answer them.
