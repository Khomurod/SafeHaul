02 · DQ FILE

See what is in the DQ file and what is missing

Each driver's DQ file lists which documents it holds and which are missing. A CDL, medical card or MVR can carry a valid-until date, and the file shows when it has expired or expires within 30 days.

  • Upload a missing document from the list
  • Once a day, a notice in the app when a hired driver's earliest valid-until date is within 30 days or has passed. In the app only: SafeHaul sends no emails or texts about it.
  • The list shows what the file holds. It is not a compliance check.

FAQ

Questions carriers ask.

Do drivers need an account?

No. A driver applies in a browser from the link your company shares, and signs documents in a browser without an account.

What does SafeHaul cost?

SafeHaul is a flat monthly subscription. Send us a request and we reply with the plans and prices.

Does SafeHaul order background checks?

No. SafeHaul does not order or receive background checks. You can keep an MVR in the driver's DQ file with its valid-until date.

Can other companies see our drivers?

No. Each company's records are isolated by database access rules, and every company has its own team, roles and application link.

Can we message drivers in bulk?

Recruiters can message a filtered list of candidates in bulk, by SMS and email. Each company connects its own messaging provider, and that provider bills the carrier charges. SafeHaul does not capture opt-out replies, and it has no two-way conversations.

Is SafeHaul a compliance service?

SafeHaul supports your own compliance process. It does not give legal advice.
