About SafeHaul

Driver hiring software for US trucking carriers

What SafeHaul Is

SafeHaul is web-based software that US trucking carriers use to hire drivers and keep their hiring records in one place, instead of in paper files, spreadsheets and email. It is built and operated by Safehaul Technologies LLC.

Carrier owners, recruiters and safety teams sign in at app.safehaul.io. Drivers fill in their application in a browser, and documents can be signed in a browser without an account.

What It Does

  • Driver applications: SafeHaul collects a complete driver application in one structured record. Applications can be submitted by an invited driver or through a public company link, and an application in progress survives a lost connection and is submitted when the device reconnects.
  • Licence photo auto-fill: A driver can start an application by photographing their licence. The auto-filled values are shown to the driver for review before submission.
  • Hiring pipeline: Recruiters move candidates through hiring stages, and leads can be added by hand or imported from a spreadsheet.
  • Driver documents: Driver documents are stored per driver, with access limited to that company's team. Each driver's DQ file lists which documents it holds and which are missing.
  • Electronic signatures: Documents can be sent for signature, and a completed document is sealed so later alteration is detectable.
  • Previous-employment verification: SafeHaul sends previous-employment verification requests to a driver's former employers and records the responses.
  • Reporting: SafeHaul reports on hiring pipeline activity from the records it holds.

How It Protects Records

SafeHaul holds hiring records on each carrier's behalf, so it keeps them apart and keeps a record of what is done with them:

  • Each company's records are isolated by database access rules, and every company has its own team, roles and public application link.
  • Document links are issued by the server and checked against company membership.
  • SafeHaul keeps activity records for actions taken on a driver record.

Google Account Access

SafeHaul asks for access to a Google account only when a company admin connects the company's Gmail or Google Workspace account, so that the emails its team sends through SafeHaul come from the company's own address. These are application links, signing requests and previous-employment verification requests, and the reminders SafeHaul schedules for them.

SafeHaul asks Google for two things: the account's email address, to show which account is connected, and permission to send email from it. It does not read, store or index the mailbox. The admin can disconnect Gmail at any time in SafeHaul's settings, and SafeHaul then deletes the stored credential.

SafeHaul's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Section 5 of our Privacy Policy covers Google user data in full.
