Inspection

Someone will ask to see the file.

When they do, four things have to be in one place and agree with each other. The usual arrangement keeps each of them somewhere else.

01

The application, complete

Contact details, qualifications, licence, violations, accidents, employment history, general information, review and consent.

02

The qualification documents

Held against the driver they belong to, not in a shared drive named after whoever set it up.

03

What was signed, and by whom

The completed document, sealed, with the record of who signed it and when.

04

What past employers answered

The requests that went out and the responses that came back, rather than a memory of a phone call.

02

Platform

One platform, from first contact to a complete driver file.

Every capability below is in the product today.

01

Driver applicant tracking

A nine-step application covering contact details, qualifications, licence, violations, accidents, employment history, general information, review and consent — with a recruiter pipeline that moves candidates through your hiring stages.

02

Documents, per driver

Qualification paperwork stored against the driver it belongs to, reachable only through server-issued links that check company membership first.

03

Electronic signature, included

Send a document, have it signed in a browser without an account, and get a sealed copy back. No separate e-signature subscription, no per-envelope charge.

04

Previous-employment verification

Requests go to past employers with tracked responses, and the results stay behind a link checked against your company.

05

Start from a licence photo

A driver photographs their CDL and the application fills itself in. Every extracted value is shown to them to check before anything is submitted.

06

Reporting on your pipeline

Where candidates enter and where they drop out, built from counters maintained as activity happens rather than recomputed on demand.

03

Apply

The application survives a bad signal.

Applications are queued in the browser and retried automatically when the connection comes back. Each one carries an identifier derived from the company, email and phone, so a retry cannot create a second copy of the same driver.

  • Invited drivers or a public application link for your company
  • Custom questions per company, on top of the standard steps
  • Work in progress is never lost to a dropped connection

04

Sign

Paperwork signed and sealed, with no envelope bill.

Build a document from a template, place the signer fields, and send it. The driver signs in a browser without creating an account, and the finished document is sealed so a later alteration is detectable.

  • Draw or type a signature; a signing link cannot be submitted twice
  • SafeHaul can suggest where signer fields belong on an uploaded form, for a person to review
  • Unlimited documents on both plans

05

Verify

A record of what was asked, and what came back.

Previous-employment verification requests are sent from SafeHaul and their responses recorded against the driver, with a built-in reminder cycle and rate limiting on the portal past employers use.

  • Result files released only through a server-issued link checked against your company
  • Activity records for actions taken on a driver record
  • Credential access is audited without ever recording the credential

SafeHaul stores and organises qualification documents. It does not track their dates for you — that stays your process, and we would rather say so than imply a safety net that is not there.

06

Mechanism

What holds up under inspection.

Four mechanisms, not four adjectives.

01

Companies are separated by the database

Cross-company access is denied by database access rules, not only by what the interface chooses to show.

02

Signed documents are tamper-evident

A completed document is sealed, so later alteration can be detected rather than argued about.

03

Files are never public URLs

Every document link is issued by the server for one request and checked against company membership before release.

04

Actions leave a trail

Activity records on applications and leads, and audited credential access that records who looked without storing what they saw.
